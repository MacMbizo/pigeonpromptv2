# AI Prompt Catalogue - Product Requirements Document

## Elevator Pitch
A community-driven AI prompt catalogue that helps English-speaking users discover, create, and share high-quality prompts for various AI tools, with intelligent categorization, voting systems, and future AI-powered prompt generation to maximize AI tool effectiveness across all major AI platforms.

## Problem Statement
- Users struggle to craft effective prompts for AI tools, leading to poor results and frustration
- Existing prompt resources are scattered, unorganized, and lack quality validation
- No central hub exists for discovering proven, community-tested prompts across different AI platforms
- Users waste time reinventing prompts that others have already perfected

## Target Audience
**Primary:** AI enthusiasts, content creators, marketers, developers, and professionals in English-speaking markets who regularly use AI tools

**Secondary:** Businesses looking to standardize AI workflows, educators using AI tools, and prompt engineers

## Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
The first comprehensive, platform-agnostic, community-curated AI prompt library with intelligent categorization, quality validation through voting, and a carefully curated starting collection of proven prompts.

## Target Platforms
- **Web application** (primary)
- **Chrome browser extension** (Phase 2 - highly recommended)
- **Mobile app** (future consideration)
- **API for integrations** (Phase 3)

## Balanced Community Guidelines Strategy

### Quality Standards:
**Minimum Requirements (not strict barriers):**
- Clear prompt description
- At least one example output
- Specified compatible AI platforms
- Proper categorization

### Three-tier Moderation System:
1. **Automated Screening**
   - Filter spam/inappropriate content

2. **Community Review**
   - 48-hour community voting period for new submissions

3. **Trusted User Fast-track**
   - Experienced contributors get immediate approval

### Encouraging Participation:
- Feedback on rejected submissions with improvement suggestions
- "Needs work" status instead of outright rejection
- Mentorship program pairing new users with experienced contributors
- Recognition for helpful reviewers

### User Confidence Measures:
- Verification badges for tested prompts
- Platform-specific success rates displayed
- Community comments and tips
- "Recently tested" timestamps
